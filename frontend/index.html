Dependency Attack Surface

              
Schedule separate audits for the top eligible dependencies ranked by recorded signals whose source is available in a verified local snapshot. Import proximity is a lead, not proof of reachability.

              

                
                
              

              
The maximum selects the top N eligible child audits. It does not limit the dependency declarations or captured dependency files visible in Sources. Captured code, scheduled audits and completed review are separate states.

              

              
Children use the normal audit queue and concurrency limit. Each has its own results and coverage. Cancelling the parent cancels its active children; child pause and resume are independent.
